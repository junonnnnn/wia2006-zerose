3.0 · Technical

Technical Risks

Four risk factors, led by how the 3D model is made.

01
Most significant

Approximate Geometry

Traced from schematic drawings, not survey data: curved walls, door widths and some labels are approximate.

02

Incorrect Routes

Wrong geometry can give wrong routes, especially stair-avoidance routes for OKU users.

03

Slow 3D Performance

The 3D view may lag on low-end phones.

04

Messy Source Drawings

Some rooms are unnamed or labelled twice on the original drawings.
