III · Justification

Why This Methodology

01

Structural Alignment

The four phases follow academic project frameworks, so every deliverable, like the SRDS report, arrives in order.

02

Risk Mitigation

Waterfall is too rigid for 3D mapping. Agile catches core logic failures early, not at final testing.

03

Incremental Testing

Short sprints build, test and fix the map and routing one piece at a time.

Plan thoroughly, then build in sprints. Planning first prevents costly errors.
